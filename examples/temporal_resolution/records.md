# Paired temporal-resolution evidence

The `prescribed-temporal-data-v1` archive accompanies the transformer temporal
check. Its two targets were fixed before optimization: nominal target 7 and
target 15, selected by maximum weighted desired-temperature exceedance. Each
target uses 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 optimization slabs over 600 seconds.

The original advective discretization and the separate half-divergence transport
form use identical thermal inputs and target functions. The latter changes the
thermal discretization; it does not replace the original solver timing evidence.
Neither population measures a fully coupled flow–temperature optimizer.

All ten corrected-formulation optimizations meet the residual, optimality and
forward-recovery criteria. The final unchanged-source replays give:

| Target | Optimization slabs | Maximum violation (K) | Final consecutive-grid change (K) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Nominal | 4 | 3.01338 | 0.01867 |
| Nominal | 64 | 0.37388 | 0.00751 |
| Demanding | 4 | 4.93853 | 0.01378 |
| Demanding | 64 | 0.41055 | 0.00570 |

All four histories meet the predeclared temporal-sensitivity criterion. Their
remaining violations show why feasibility at the optimization time levels
must be distinguished from the finer forward response.

## Contents

`data/original` and `data/corrected` contain the two predeclared designs, all
twenty optimization records and their summaries. Every unsuccessful attempt
remains present. The summary's `verified` field includes the forward-recovery
check; a solver status of `converged` alone does not establish that check.

`data/stability.json` contains the computed amplification modes for both forms
at all seven time steps, including the original four-step eigenpair that fails
its residual test. A growing verified mode establishes instability; the sparse
calculation of decaying modes does not certify the complete spectrum.

`data/replays` contains every initial, extended and final forward assessment for
the four preselected source fields: both targets at four and 64 optimization
slabs. The final grids have 16384, 32768 and 65536 steps. Each source remains
piecewise constant on its original intervals. No control is changed and no
temperature is clipped. Earlier resolution caps remain in the archive.

The maximum change between each of the last two pairs of forward grids must be
at most 0.05 K, and every independently evaluated equation residual must be at
most `1e-10`. This is a fixed-mesh temporal-sensitivity criterion, separate from
temperature-bound satisfaction. The reported maximum violations include every
forward time level, with their locations and times. They do not bound values
at every point in continuous time or establish spatial resolution.

The SHA-256 manifest covers the raw scalar records. The archive also identifies
the source version and checksum of every optimized field. Full field arrays are
regenerated by the [versioned numerical commands](README.md); scalar summaries
and figures can be regenerated directly from the archive. Those commands use
the measured sources, rather than the latest numerical implementation.

## Regenerate the figures and summaries

Use the `prescribed-temporal-data-v1` checkout with the frozen study and plotting
dependencies. The following command reads only the archived scalar records:

```bash
uv sync --frozen --extra study --extra plot
uv run --frozen python tools/report_temporal_assessment.py \
  --original examples/temporal_resolution/data/original/summary.json \
  --corrected examples/temporal_resolution/data/corrected/summary.json \
  --stability examples/temporal_resolution/data/stability.json \
  --replays examples/temporal_resolution/data/replays/final/case-00.json \
            examples/temporal_resolution/data/replays/final/case-04.json \
            examples/temporal_resolution/data/replays/final/case-05.json \
            examples/temporal_resolution/data/replays/final/case-09.json \
  --output runs/prescribed-temporal-figures
```

Replace `final` with `initial` or `extension` and choose a new output directory
to inspect the preceding resolution stages. The reporting code checks that the
physical inputs and optimization settings match apart from the declared
transport form and that all four selected replays remain present.

The [repository-only compact figure](../development_studies/README.md#temporal-resolution-pilot)
combines the optimized-state differences with the final fixed-source bound
checks. Its reproduction command is given alongside that figure; the detailed
plots above retain all forward grids and the amplification diagnostics.
